Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

SlipComposer (“the app”, “we”, “us”) provides statistical and market-consensus sports information for entertainment purposes. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. SlipComposer is not a betting operator, does not accept wagers, and does not handle real-money gambling.

1.Information we collect

  • Account identifier. When you choose to sign in, our authentication provider (Google Firebase Authentication) processes your sign-in identifier — for example your email address, or an Apple/Google account token when you use “Sign in with Apple” or Google Sign-In. You can use core features without signing in.
  • Usage and diagnostic data. Basic, non-identifying information about how the app is used — screens opened, features used — collected through Google Firebase Analytics, and crash reports collected through Firebase Crashlytics, to operate and improve the service. Usage analytics are collected only with your permission and can be turned off at any time in the app under Profile → Share anonymous usage data. This data is never used for advertising and is never shared with betting operators.
  • Advertising. The free tier of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only. The app does not ask for permission to track you, does not use the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not build an advertising profile of you. To serve and cap ads, and to measure whether an ad installation happened, Google may process device and ad-interaction information as described in its own policies. Where the EEA, the UK or Switzerland requires it, a Google-certified consent form is shown before any ad request is made, and your choice is stored on your device.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive a confirmation/receipt to grant your entitlement; we do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • On-device data. Your saved slips, diamond balance, followed teams/leagues, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device unless you are signed in and it is synced to provide the service.

2.How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the app and its features.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account and entitlements consistent.
  • To process in-app purchases and activate premium features.
  • To diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and ensure security.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3.How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers that operate core functions on our behalf — for example Google Firebase (authentication, analytics, crash reporting), Google AdMob (advertising), and our sports-data and hosting providers
  • App stores (Apple, Google) to process purchases.
  • Legal authorities when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service.

4.Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. On-device data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — in the app under Profile → Delete account, or by contacting us below.

5.Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, or to delete your account, contact us at the address below.

6.Children

SlipComposer is intended for adults and is not directed at children. The app presents sports statistics and market-consensus information about betting markets; it contains no gambling and no simulated gambling, takes no wagers, and holds no funds. It is rated for adults accordingly. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7.Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8.International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
